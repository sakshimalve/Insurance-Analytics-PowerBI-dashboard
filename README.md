# 📊 Insurance Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Insurance Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze insurance policy, customer, premium, and claim data.

The dashboard transforms raw insurance data into meaningful visual insights that help understand business performance, customer behavior, policy trends, and claim patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze insurance policy performance.
- Track total premiums and claim amounts.
- Understand customer demographics.
- Analyze claim approval and rejection trends.
- Compare policy performance across different categories.
- Identify important business trends and patterns.
- Create an interactive and user-friendly business intelligence dashboard.

---

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
